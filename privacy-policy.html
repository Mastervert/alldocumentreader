All documents reader Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Overview

All documents reader (“the app”) is a document viewer and organizer that works mainly on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses, collects, uses and shares. It applies to the app published by Raphael (“we”, “us”).

What stays on your device

Your documents, folders, classifications, settings and reading preferences are stored on your device. We do not upload your documents. The app has no user accounts. In this version we do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Information we collect

Feedback you choose to send. If you use Feedback & Suggestions, we receive the message you type, the category you pick (Suggestion, Problem or Other), the app version and the app language. Please do not put sensitive personal information in your message.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location through the app. If you write to us from your own email app, we receive the email address you send from.

Permissions and why we ask

Files and documents: to open, organize, convert, merge, split, sign, lock and unlock the documents you choose. We only access files you select or folders you allow.

Camera: only when you use Scan to PDF. Photos are turned into a PDF on your device and are not uploaded.

Internet: only to send the feedback you submit. Your documents are never sent.

Bluetooth, nearby devices and sharing: when you share a document, Android’s share sheet sends it to the app or device you choose. We do not receive it.

Printing: sends the document to the print service you choose.

How your documents are handled

Converting, merging, splitting, signing and locking all happen on your device. When you lock a document, the password is not stored; instead a salted, one-way hash is kept on the device and the password is checked against it. We cannot recover a forgotten password.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Feedback you send is delivered to our email inbox through Google’s email service. When you share a document or the app link, the app or service you choose receives it and handles it under its own policy.

If a paid plan is added, purchases are processed by Google Play and we do not see your payment details.

Security

Feedback is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Data on your device is protected by your phone’s own security. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Feedback emails are kept only as long as we need them to improve the app. To ask us to delete feedback you sent, email us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices and rights

You can stop any access by removing permissions in your phone’s settings, and you can delete all app data by uninstalling the app. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. Contact us to use these rights.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, the effective date above will change, and we will tell you in the app about significant changes.
